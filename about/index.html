Our mission

Make online discovery simpler and more accessible.

6ixo brings several everyday needs into one marketplace so users can compare relevant local options without moving between unrelated platforms. The goal is to support useful connections while giving users clear information and practical safety tools.

For buyers and renters

Search by location and category, review listing details and ask the seller or host direct questions before making a decision.

For sellers and hosts

Create accurate listings with current photos, honest descriptions, transparent prices and clear transaction or booking terms.

For local businesses

Present relevant services and inventory to people actively searching in the areas those businesses serve.

How the marketplace works

6ixo connects people; users verify each opportunity.

6ixo provides discovery, listing and communication tools. Unless a specific checkout or booking flow states otherwise, the seller, buyer, host, renter or service provider remains responsible for verifying identity, condition, documents, terms and payment arrangements.

Search or publish

Browse categories and locations, or create an accurate listing for an item, property, vehicle or service.

Compare and communicate

Review the complete details and use direct questions to clarify condition, availability, price and expectations.

Verify before committing

Inspect what is being offered, confirm relevant documents and use a payment or booking process that creates a record.

Transparency

A marketplace earns trust over time.

6ixo is growing and does not claim millions of users, guaranteed verification or guaranteed transaction outcomes. We are working to build useful inventory and improve safety, reporting and marketplace tools as the platform develops.

Questions, corrections and partnership inquiries can be sent to contact@6ixo.com.
