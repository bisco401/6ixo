Account and data control

Delete your 6ixo account

You can request permanent deletion of your 6ixo account and personal information associated with it.

Last updated: September 30, 2026
01

How to request deletion

  1. Email contact@6ixo.com from the email address connected to your 6ixo account.
  2. Use the subject line 6ixo account deletion request.
  3. Include your public username, if you have one. Do not send your password, payment-card number or a copy of identification unless we specifically request a secure verification method.
Start deletion request
02

What we delete

After verification, we will delete or de-identify personal information associated with your account from active systems, including your account profile, private preferences, uploaded profile media, active listings and other user content, subject to the exceptions below.

Deleting the app from your device does not delete your 6ixo account. You must submit a deletion request.

03

Information that may be retained

We may retain limited information where permitted or required for completed transactions, tax and accounting obligations, chargebacks, fraud prevention, safety investigations, enforcement of our rules, dispute resolution or legal claims. We will not use retained information for unrelated marketing.

Residual copies may remain temporarily in protected backups until the backups are overwritten through the normal cycle. Content copied or reshared by another person is outside our direct control.

04

Verification and timing

We may ask you to verify control of the account so that another person cannot delete it without permission. We will acknowledge and complete verified requests within the period required by applicable law and will explain if an exception applies.

For additional information, read the 6ixo Privacy Policy or contact the Privacy Officer at contact@6ixo.com.
