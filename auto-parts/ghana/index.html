Frequently asked questions

Auto Parts questions for Ghana

How do I find the correct auto part in Ghana?

Search with the vehicle year, make, model, engine and exact part number. Use the VIN where a qualified seller or technician recommends it.

Are used car parts available?

Listings may include new, refurbished and used parts. Ask how a used part was tested and whether any return protection applies.

Can a similar-looking part fit my car?

Not necessarily. Connectors, software, dimensions and specifications can differ even when parts look alike. Confirm the exact identifiers.

Should a mechanic install the part?

Qualified installation is especially important for brakes, steering, suspension, airbags, electrical modules and complex engine components.
