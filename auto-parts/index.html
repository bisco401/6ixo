Avoid the wrong fit

Three checks before buying a car part

Identify the exact part

Use the VIN when appropriate, record the OEM or manufacturer number and compare photos.

Confirm condition and fit

Ask about wear, damage, testing, included hardware and the specific vehicles it fits.

Plan installation

Know whether the work needs a qualified technician, special tools or additional components.

Buy carefully

Compatibility comes first

  • Do not rely on a similar appearance alone; verify the part number and specifications.
  • Ask whether used electrical and mechanical components have been tested.
  • Follow local rules for safety-critical parts and use qualified installation when needed.
