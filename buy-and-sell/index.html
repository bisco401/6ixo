Marketplace safety

Keep the exchange safe

  • Meet in a well-lit public place and let someone know where you are going for an in-person exchange.
  • Avoid overpayment arrangements, gift-card requests and pressure to use an unfamiliar payment service.
  • Do not share one-time passwords, account credentials or unnecessary identity documents with a buyer or seller.
