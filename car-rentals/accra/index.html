Rent with confidence

Before picking up a rental car

  • Confirm the total price, refundable deposit, insurance, mileage and fuel arrangement.
  • Verify the provider and make sure the vehicle and driver documents meet the rental requirements.
  • Photograph the exterior, interior, fuel level and existing damage at pickup and return.
  • Save the provider's contact details and ask what support is available if the vehicle has a problem.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about renting a car in Accra

Can I arrange a rental at Accra airport?

Some providers may deliver to Kotoka International Airport. Confirm the terminal, flight time, meeting point and delivery fee.

Can I rent a car with a driver?

Some listings may offer a driver. Confirm working hours, waiting time, fuel and travel outside Greater Accra.

How much does an Accra rental cost?

Pricing varies by vehicle, dates and service. Compare the complete quote, including deposit, insurance and additional fees.

Can the vehicle leave Accra?

Ask the provider before booking. Some agreements may limit destinations or require advance notice for longer trips.

What documents should I bring?

Confirm the provider's licence, age, identification, payment and deposit requirements before pickup.

What vehicle should I choose?

Choose based on passengers, luggage and the full route. A compact car, sedan, SUV or van may fit different trips.
