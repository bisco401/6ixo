Before confirming

Ghana car rental checklist

  • Verify the provider, vehicle registration, driver requirements and insurance coverage.
  • Ask for the total cost including taxes, deposit, fuel, mileage, delivery and driver charges.
  • Inspect the vehicle, photograph its condition and record the fuel level at pickup and return.
  • Get written permission before cross-border travel or travel into any area excluded by the agreement.

Frequently asked questions

Car rental questions for Ghana

What do I need to rent a car in Ghana?

Requirements vary. Confirm the accepted driving licence, minimum age, ID or passport, deposit, insurance and payment method with the provider.

Can I rent a car with a driver?

Some listings may include a driver or chauffeur. Ask what the quote covers, especially fuel, waiting time and overnight travel.

Can a rental car be delivered to the airport?

Some providers may arrange airport pickup or delivery. Confirm the terminal, meeting point, arrival time and delivery fee.

What should I compare besides the daily price?

Compare the total charge, deposit, mileage, fuel, insurance, delivery, late-return and cancellation rules.

Can I drive the rental outside Ghana?

Never assume cross-border use is permitted. You may need written authorization, additional insurance and destination-specific documents.

Should I choose a car or an SUV?

Base the choice on passengers, luggage, route and expected road conditions. Ask the host about the specific journey before booking.
