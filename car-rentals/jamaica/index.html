Frequently asked questions

Car Rentals questions for Jamaica

What do I need to rent a car in Jamaica?

Requirements vary by provider. Confirm the accepted driving licence, age, identification, payment, deposit and insurance before booking.

Can I find a rental car with a driver?

Some listings may offer a driver or chauffeur. Confirm working hours, fuel, waiting time and travel outside the pickup city.

How should I compare rental prices?

Compare the complete quote for your dates, including deposit, insurance, mileage, fuel, delivery, driver and late-return charges.

Can I arrange airport pickup?

Some providers may offer airport delivery. Confirm the airport, terminal, arrival time, meeting point and additional fee.
