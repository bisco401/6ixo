Frequently asked questions

Cars for Sale questions for Accra

How do I find used cars for sale in Accra?

Search by body style, price and location, then compare current listings from private owners and dealers.

Should I inspect a car before buying?

Yes. A qualified independent inspection can identify mechanical, electrical, accident and maintenance issues that are not obvious in photos.

What documents should match the car?

Check that the vehicle identifiers and registration details match the physical car and that the seller is authorized to transfer ownership.

Is the advertised price the full cost?

Not always. Budget for inspection, transfer, registration, insurance, servicing and any repairs needed after purchase.
