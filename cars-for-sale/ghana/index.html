Frequently asked questions

Cars for Sale questions for Ghana

Where can I find cars for sale in Ghana?

6ixo can organize owner and dealer listings by location. Availability depends on current listings, so search the city and vehicle type you need.

What should I check before buying a used car?

Verify ownership and vehicle identifiers, review service and repair information, road-test the car and arrange an independent inspection.

Can I buy directly from the owner?

Some listings may come from private owners and others from dealers. Confirm the seller’s identity and legal authority to complete the sale.

What costs should I budget for besides the price?

Plan for inspection, ownership transfer, registration, insurance, servicing and any immediate repairs or replacement parts.
