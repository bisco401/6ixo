Marketplace safety

Protect the transaction

  • Be cautious of prices far below comparable vehicles or sellers who refuse an inspection.
  • Verify the title, registration and seller identity using the rules in your location.
  • Use a traceable payment method and never pay for a vehicle you cannot verify.
