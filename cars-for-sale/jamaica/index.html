Where can I find cars for sale in Jamaica?

What should I check before buying a used car?

Verify ownership and identifiers, review history and maintenance information, road-test the car and arrange an independent inspection.

Can I buy directly from an owner?

Some listings may be private and others dealer-listed. Confirm the seller’s identity and authority to complete the transfer.
