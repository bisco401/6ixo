Community safety

Use good judgment when connecting.

Community posts come from users. Verify important details independently before attending, paying or sharing personal information.

  • Confirm the organizer and location through reliable sources.
  • Use secure payment methods and avoid urgent off-platform requests.
  • Meet in public, tell someone your plans and report suspicious content.
