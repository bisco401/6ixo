Check before buying

A smarter used-electronics checklist

Confirm the model

Compare the exact model number, storage, network compatibility and included accessories.

Test core functions

Check charging, display, cameras, ports, connectivity and battery health when possible.

Verify ownership

Ask for proof of purchase, make sure activation locks are removed and record the transaction.

Marketplace safety

Avoid locked or misrepresented devices

  • Verify serial or device identifiers through appropriate official channels when available.
  • Do not buy a phone or computer that remains tied to someone else's account.
  • Meet safely, inspect the item and be cautious of pressure to pay before testing.
