Event safety

Confirm details before attending.

Listings and community posts may change. Verify important information with the venue or organizer and use secure payment methods for tickets.

  • Be cautious of pressure to pay through unusual or irreversible methods.
  • Keep tickets and personal information private.
  • For unfamiliar locations, share your plans and arrange safe transportation.
