Make a confident choice

Buy and sell with clearer expectations

Check measurements

Compare actual garment and footwear measurements with an item that already fits.

Review condition

Look for clear photos of labels, seams, soles, hardware, stains and signs of wear.

Confirm authenticity

For higher-value items, request provenance and use an independent authentication option when appropriate.

Marketplace safety

Know what you are buying

  • Be cautious when a luxury or collectible item is priced far below comparable listings.
  • Ask for original photos and specific detail shots instead of relying only on catalog images.
  • Use a payment method with a transaction record and understand the return agreement before paying.
