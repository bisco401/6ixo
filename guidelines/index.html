1. Where these rules apply

These rules apply to accounts, profiles, listings, reviews, messages, community and reward posts, dating interactions and other 6ixo features. Conduct away from 6ixo may also lead to action when it involves people you met here and creates a credible safety risk.

Adults aged 18 or older may use interactive features. Businesses and their representatives must identify themselves accurately. Follow the Terms of Use and applicable law, and use the Listing Rules when advertising. Hosts and guests must also follow the Short-Term Rental Guidelines for stays, including house rules, privacy and reporting.

2. Be honest and respect other people

  • Use your own identity and authorized images. Do not impersonate a person, business or 6ixo staff, create fake verification, or conceal a commercial offer as a personal profile.
  • Do not scam, phish, blackmail, request passwords or codes, manipulate reviews, make fake bookings, or use accounts and payment claims to deceive people.
  • Do not threaten, stalk, bully or harass anyone. Stop contacting a person when they decline, block you or ask you to stop. Creating another account to contact them is also prohibited.
  • Do not publish hate speech, slurs or calls for exclusion or violence based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or another protected characteristic. Listings and hiring or housing decisions must comply with applicable non-discrimination law.
  • Do not spam, mass-message, evade bans, submit malicious reports or interfere with another person’s account or device.

3. Protect privacy and use your own content

Do not publish another person’s private address, live whereabouts, phone number, identity documents, financial details, private messages or other private information without permission. Do not use a reward or community post to target a person, encourage surveillance, make unverified accusations or organize vigilante action.

Post only material you own or are authorized to use. Do not share intimate images, sexual recordings or manipulated sexual images of an identifiable person without their explicit permission. Threatening to disclose such material is prohibited. Permission to send something privately is not permission to publish it.

4. Dating, companionship and consent

Dating and companionship features are for adults seeking voluntary personal connections. A match, profile label, subscription, gift or previous encounter is not consent to a message, image, meeting or sexual activity. Consent must be freely given and can be withdrawn at any time.

  • No nudity, pornography or explicit sexual imagery in public profiles, listings or posts.
  • No unsolicited sexual messages or media. Private adult conversations must be welcome to everyone involved and must stop when consent is withdrawn.
  • No coercion, sextortion, stalking, intoxication-based exploitation, trafficking, sexual services for compensation, escort advertising or compensated sexual arrangements.
  • Do not use a massage, job, service or travel listing to disguise a paid sexual offer. Legitimate professional services belong in their appropriate marketplace category.
  • Never use another person’s identity, AI-generated persona or verification claim to mislead someone about who they are meeting.

Meetings are optional. Use public places, tell someone your plans and retain control of transport and documents. Read our meeting safety guidance.

5. Child safety and exploitation

People under 18 may not create accounts or use interactive features. Do not seek contact with minors through 6ixo or misrepresent an age.

Sexual exploitation or abuse of children, grooming, sexual material involving or depicting minors, and attempts to trade or obtain that material are prohibited, including synthetic or manipulated material. Human trafficking and any offer that exploits a person are prohibited. Report suspected child exploitation with the account or listing reference; do not download, forward or attach abusive material to a report. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

6. Dangerous content and misleading claims

Do not incite violence, glorify terrorism, encourage self-harm, publish graphic abuse, sell prohibited weapons, or solicit illegal or dangerous acts. Do not publish false claims about insurance, licences, certifications, availability, medical outcomes or financial returns.

Reward posts must describe a legitimate request for information or the recovery of property. Do not offer rewards to harm, threaten, detain or unlawfully track someone. Do not expose a private person’s sensitive details or present an accusation as an established fact. Send urgent crime or missing-person information to the appropriate authorities.

7. Reporting, review and appeals

Use Report on the relevant profile, listing or chat where available. You can also email contact@6ixo.com with “Safety report” in the subject. Include the URL or reference, public username, what happened and when, and relevant non-sensitive screenshots or messages. Report a suspected copyright or privacy violation with evidence of your rights or authority to act. Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers or copies of identity documents.

We assess reports against these rules and the evidence available. Depending on severity and repeated conduct, we may request a correction, warn an account, remove or reduce visibility of content, restrict a feature, or suspend or terminate an account. Severe harm, fraud or a legal requirement may require immediate action without a prior warning. A paid placement is subject to the same rules.

Where reasonably possible, an affected user is told the relevant rule and action. We may withhold identifying or investigative details to protect people, privacy or legal obligations. Reporting information is shared only where needed for review, safety or law; we cannot promise absolute confidentiality.

If you believe an action was mistaken, email contact@6ixo.com with “Moderation appeal” in the subject. Include your account email, public username, affected content or decision and any explanation or new evidence. We will review the decision and reply with the outcome or request more information. We aim to acknowledge emailed reports and appeals within three business days; complex investigations may take longer, and applicable legal deadlines take priority. If you have not received an acknowledgement, follow up using the same subject and references.

Disagreeing with a price or receiving a rejected offer is not itself a policy violation. Do not use reports to retaliate. 6ixo is not an emergency monitoring service; contact emergency services, your bank or the relevant authority when immediate action is needed.
