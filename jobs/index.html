Marketplace safety

Watch for employment scams

  • Be cautious of guaranteed income, vague duties or pressure to move the conversation off-platform immediately.
  • Do not deposit a cheque and return part of the money or purchase equipment from an unknown seller.
  • Confirm interviews, offers and onboarding instructions through a verified company channel.
