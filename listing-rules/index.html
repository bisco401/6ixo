Listing Rules

What you may list, what is prohibited, and the details every offer must disclose.

1. Every listing must be accurate

  • List only something you own or are authorized to offer. Use the correct category and real location, and identify a business, dealer or agent accurately.
  • Use clear current descriptions and photographs that you are entitled to publish. Disclose defects, condition, availability and material restrictions. Do not imply that an illustrative or stock photo shows the actual item.
  • Show the actual price, currency and what it buys. Disclose unavoidable charges, deposits, delivery or cleaning fees, taxes, recurring charges and cancellation or refund terms before a person commits.
  • Remove or update unavailable listings. Do not post deceptive duplicates, irrelevant keywords, bait prices, false discounts, forged verification or fake reviews.
  • Follow laws that apply to the seller, buyer, item, service and transaction location. A listing being visible on 6ixo does not make it legal or establish that it has been checked.

2. Prohibited goods, services and offers

Do not list, request, link to or facilitate any of the following:

  • Stolen or counterfeit goods, pirated media, unauthorized copyrighted material, stolen accounts, credentials, identity documents or personal-data lists.
  • Weapons intended to injure people, firearms, ammunition, explosives, or instructions or services for illegal harm.
  • Illegal drugs, controlled substances, prescription medicines, tobacco or vaping products, or offers to evade age, licensing or import restrictions.
  • Sexual services for compensation, escort offers, compensated sexual arrangements, trafficking, exploitation, pornography, or intimate media shared without consent.
  • Child sexual exploitation material, grooming, child trafficking or any sexualized offer involving a minor.
  • Scams, pyramid schemes, fake investments, phishing, forged documents, fake qualifications, money-laundering services or guaranteed financial returns.
  • Recalled or demonstrably unsafe products; illegal wildlife products, protected species or offers that involve animal cruelty.
  • Discriminatory, threatening or harassing offers, private personal information without permission, or rewards for harm, unlawful detention or surveillance.

Attempts to disguise a prohibited offer through abbreviations, images, external links or a different category are also prohibited.

3. Regulated goods and professional services

An item or service not listed above is not automatically permitted. Where an otherwise permitted offer requires a licence, registration, authorization, age restriction, safety inspection or product labelling, the provider must meet those requirements and explain material eligibility limits. 6ixo may request supporting information or decline a high-risk listing.

Do not claim to provide licensed healthcare, legal, financial, transport, childcare or another regulated service without the required authority. Do not promise guaranteed medical results or earnings. Do not use a legitimate service description to disguise a prohibited activity.

4. Property and short-term stays

Owners, hosts and agents must have authority to offer the property and meet local rental, accommodation, permit, safety and tax requirements. Describe the real property, room type, capacity, amenities, accessibility, availability, house rules, location and price. Explain cleaning fees, deposits and cancellation arrangements before commitment.

Do not use fake addresses, copied photographs of another property, fabricated availability or discriminatory eligibility conditions. Protect guests’ and occupants’ privacy: do not publicly post identity documents, access codes or private contact details. Approved host status does not replace legal permits or property checks. Hosts and guests must follow the Short-Term Rental Guidelines, including cleaning and access standards, guest conduct, camera restrictions and permission for host entry. Platform booking terms are described in the rental Terms.

5. Vehicles, rentals and parts

Vehicle sellers must describe ownership or authority, make, model, year, condition, material defects and any known title or history issue accurately. Do not misrepresent mileage, accident history, registration or inspection status. Parts and accessories must state relevant fitment and condition without unsupported safety claims.

Rental hosts must have authority to rent the vehicle and disclose actual rental coverage, exclusions and deductibles, driver eligibility, daily rate, pickup and return times, included distance, excess-distance charges, delivery fees, any deposit and cancellation rules. Do not describe host-provided insurance as a 6ixo protection plan. Drivers must meet the listing’s licence and age requirements.

6. Jobs, services and events

Job listings must identify a real employer or authorized recruiter, describe the work, pay or compensation basis, location, hours, material requirements and employment or contractor status accurately. No upfront application payment scams, fake recruitment, unlawful discrimination or sexual services disguised as a job.

Service providers and event organizers must accurately disclose their identity, qualifications, location, scope, availability, price, cancellation terms and any required permits. Do not imply a medical, professional, safety or authenticity certification that you cannot substantiate. Paid business offers belong in marketplace categories rather than personal dating profiles.

7. Imported listings and rights complaints

Some offers are supplied by partners or displayed from public listing sources where collection and display are permitted. A source link is a reference, not proof that the listing is current, the source endorses 6ixo, or the provider is a registered 6ixo user. Verify current terms with the source provider before transacting.

If a listing contains your content or personal information without appropriate permission, email contact@6ixo.com with “Listing rights request” in the subject, the 6ixo URL or listing reference, the original source, the material affected and evidence of ownership or authority. We will review the request and may correct, restrict or remove the material. Do not attach identity documents unless we provide a secure verification method.

8. Removal, corrections and review

Listings that violate these rules may be corrected, removed or limited in visibility, and accounts may face restrictions under the reporting and appeals process. Payment for promotion does not exempt a listing from review.

Use Report where available or email contact@6ixo.com with the listing reference and concern. To challenge a removal, use “Moderation appeal” and explain the decision you believe is mistaken. Review our Safety guidance before paying, sharing documents or arranging a meeting.
