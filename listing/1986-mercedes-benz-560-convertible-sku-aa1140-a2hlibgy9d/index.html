In 1986, Mercedes-Benz shipped its most powerful SL of the decade to the U.S. in the 560SL. The new 5.5-liter V-8 engine’s 227 hp improved top speed from 110 mph in the 380SL to 130 mph, and the car was quicker from a standstill to 60 mph by about 4 full seconds.

The engine was much torquier, too, which greatly improved the cars’ dynamics. Likewise, the car received a new rear suspension and a limited-slip differential to better handle the added torque and power. Mercedes-Benz also added anti-lock brakes, leather upholstery, an alarm system, and an air bag.
