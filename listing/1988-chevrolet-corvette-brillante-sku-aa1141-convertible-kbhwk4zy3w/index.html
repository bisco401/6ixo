Combining advanced American technology with European styling, Classic International Automotive Styling Center created the ultimate driving machine. The Brillante.

Nick and his friend, Ted Carlson, founded Classic Limousine in 1984 and became known for their innovative styling. They opened the Classic International Automotive Styling Center where they gained a good reputation in the field of unusual conversions.
