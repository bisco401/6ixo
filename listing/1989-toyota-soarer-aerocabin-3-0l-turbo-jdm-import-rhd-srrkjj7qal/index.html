1989 Toyota Soarer Aerocabin, 3.0L Turbo, Automatic Transmission, 2WD, 99k Original Miles Verified, One of Only 500 Ever Produced. JDM Import 2026, RHD.

The Toyota Soarer Aerocabin (Z20 chassis) is one of the rarest and most unique JDM vehicles, featuring an innovative power-retractable hardtop. Because it was limited to an exclusive production run of just 500 units for the 1989 model year, it remains a highly sought-after collector's item. This rare example is becoming increasingly difficult to find. Powered by the legendary 3.0-liter turbocharged inline-six 7M-GTE, producing 232 to 240 horsepower. At the press of a switch, the power-operated metal roof and rear window retract into the trunk area. Unlike the standard Soarer, the Aerocabin is a strict two-seater.
