The Mitsubishi Galant VR-4 Type V combines understated sedan styling with serious performance, making it one of the most distinctive Japanese performance sedans of its era. Under the hood is Mitsubishi’s legendary twin-turbocharged 2.5L 6A13TT V6 engine, producing 280 PS (approximately 276 hp) and paired with a 5-speed INVECS-II automatic transmission.
