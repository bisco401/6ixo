2002 Porsche 911 Carrera Coupe finished in Guards Red over Black leather, showing just 33k miles and paired with a six-speed manual transmission.

As a 996.2, this car benefits from the important updates Porsche introduced for the facelift, including the revised front-end design with Turbo-style headlights and, more importantly, the larger 3.6-liter flat-six in place of the earlier 3.4-liter engine. The 3.6 brought more power and stronger midrange torque, helping make the later 996 Carreras some of the most satisfying cars in the generation.

This example has a very clean, straightforward specification with Carrera 2 wheels and the desirable factory Sport Exhaust, which gives the car a noticeably more purposeful sound without taking away from its usability.
