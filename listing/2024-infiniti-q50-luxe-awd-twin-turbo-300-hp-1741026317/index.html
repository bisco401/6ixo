About this listing

This 2024 Infiniti Q50 LUXE AWD is loaded with the features today's buyers are searching for, including heated front seats, a power moonroof, auto on/off LED headlights and daytime running lights, rain-sensing wipers, and the signature chrome double-arch grille paired with 17-inch aluminum alloy wheels. Inside, the cabin features Lunar Black lacquer trim and comfortable leatherette seating, giving this Q50 an upscale, refined feel from the moment you step in.

Performance comes from a 3.0L twin-turbocharged V6 producing 300 horsepower and 295 lb-ft of torque, paired with a smooth 7-speed automatic transmission and Infiniti's Intelligent All-Wheel Drive system, delivering confident handling in all weather conditions with genuine sport sedan character.

Comfort and convenience continue inside with a well-appointed cabin that balances everyday practicality with true luxury touches, making this Q50 as enjoyable to drive around town as it is on the highway.
