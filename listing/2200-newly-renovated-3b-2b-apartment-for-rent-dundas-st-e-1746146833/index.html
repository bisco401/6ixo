About this listing

1011 Dundas Street East consists of 3 apartment buildings around a communal thoroughfare with landscaped grounds and walkways leading to building entrances. Suites feature spacious layouts with in-suite laundry and private balconies.

The site’s location offers residents an easy commute to points within Mississauga and beyond via its access to major transportation thoroughfares such as Highway 427, 401, 403 and the QEW, along with MiWay (Mississauga Transit) and the GO.

Situated at the intersection of major arterial roadways, 1011 Dundas Street East benefit from excellent access throughout Mississauga via Dundas Street, Bloor Street, The Queensway,

Burnhamthorpe Road, Tomken Road and Dixie Road.
