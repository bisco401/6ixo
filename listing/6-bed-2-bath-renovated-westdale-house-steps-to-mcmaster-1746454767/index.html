About this listing

Renovated 6-bedroom, 2-bathroom detached house for lease at 44 Haddon Ave N in Westdale, a short walk to McMaster University. Available May 1, 2027.

The home was renovated top to bottom in 2023, with modern finishes throughout. The main floor has a living room, dining room and kitchen, plus a large rear living room with a big window overlooking the backyard. The six bedrooms are spread across three floors, and there's a 4-piece bathroom on the main floor and a 3-piece on the second.
