About this item

The Apple Magic Keyboard for iPad Air 11-inch is a folio keyboard case with a floating cantilever design, a built-in trackpad, a 14-key function row, and a USB-C pass-through charging port. It attaches magnetically to the iPad Air without requiring Bluetooth pairing or separate charging.

Key Features

Floating Cantilever Design: Holds the iPad Air magnetically and lets you smoothly tilt it to your preferred viewing angle while folding into a cover for front and back protectionTyping & Trackpad: Features a scissor-switch key mechanism with 1mm key travel, a 14-key top function row (for quick controls like brightness, volume, and media playback), and a glass trackpad supporting iPadOS cursor navigation and Multi-Touch gestures.

Pass-Through Charging: Includes a built-in USB-C connector for charging the iPad, leaving the iPad's own USB-C port open for accessories.

Compatible Models: iPad Air 11-inch (M4, M3, M2) and iPad Air (4th and 5th generation).
