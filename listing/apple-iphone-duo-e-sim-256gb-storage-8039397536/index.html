Apple iPhone Duo E-SIM (256GB STORAGE)

The Apple iPhone Duo is Apple's first foldable iPhone, combining a premium titanium design with two Super Retina XDR OLED displays. The 7.6-inch inner folding display provides a spacious viewing experience when the phone is opened, while the 5.4-inch outer display allows convenient everyday use when the device is closed. Both displays support ProMotion with adaptive refresh rates up to 120Hz, Always-On display, HDR, True Tone, and wide P3 colour.

Powered by the Apple A20 Pro chip with a Dual 16-core Neural Engine and advanced thermal management, the iPhone Duo is designed for demanding applications, multitasking, gaming, photography, and AI-powered features. The 256GB storage provides ample space for apps, photos, videos, documents, and personal files. The device features a 48MP Dual Fusion camera system, USB-C connectivity, Touch ID integrated into the side button, and Apple Intelligence features through iOS 27.
