AWEI WIRELESS KA25 SPEAKER

The AWEI KA25 is a home-entertainment Bluetooth soundbar system designed to bring rich, cinematic audio into your living room, gaming setup or media space. With a total rated output of around 100 W (two 25 W drivers for the soundbar plus a 50 W subwoofer), it aims to deliver deeper bass, clear mids and highs for movies, music, and games. The system supports multiple input methods — wireless Bluetooth streaming for smartphones/tablets, USB stick playback (up to 32 GB), AUX wired input, optical and HDMI inputs—all backed by a smart LED indicator system which shows the current input channel via colour.
