Rosebud Technologies have the expertise to repair and service for all makes and model of thermal barcode label printers, line printers and dot matrix printers and can repair or service them fast and efficiently at a very competitive price.

WE REPAIR THE FOLLOWING BRANDS : Honeywell, Zebra, SATO, Datamax, TSC, Datalogic, GoDex, CAB, EPSON, AML, Lexmark, Avery, Century, Novexx, Toshiba and other.

Barcode printers and scanners can experience a variety of issues that require repair. Here are some of the most common issues that we see at Rosebud Technologies:

Printed labels being faded just on the ends of the labels, Printhead misalignment, Printer not receiving sent print jobs, Miscalibrated or defective label pitch sensor, Light print or faded image, Partially missing print or cut off, Broken bars or lines in a barcode, White lines in the print, Printhead replacement, Blank labels, Ribbon and Labels skewing, Getting Errors: “Ribbon out” “Head open” “Cover open”…
