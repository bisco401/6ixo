CAEL In-Ground Single Post Car Lift H Shape Space-Saving Professional Lift with 360 Access

The CAEL In-Ground Single Post Car Lift H Shape is a premium hydraulic lifting solution designed for automotive workshops, tire shops, detailing bays, service garages, dealerships, and vehicle showrooms. Its in-ground design maximizes floor space while providing excellent access around the vehicle. Featuring a stable H-shape support platform, pneumatic-hydraulic operation, and 360 rotation capability, this lift delivers flexibility, efficiency, and a clean professional workshop appearance. With a lifting capacity of 4,000 kg (8,818 lbs), it is ideal for passenger vehicles, SUVs, and light commercial vehicles.
