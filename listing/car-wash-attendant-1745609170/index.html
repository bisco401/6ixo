Job Description

SmartShine Car Wash is looking for hardworking, reliable, and motivated individuals to join our team as Car Wash Attendants.

We are a fast-paced car wash that takes pride in providing high-quality service to our customers. We are looking for individuals who are willing to work hard, stay active throughout their shifts, and pay close attention to detail.

Responsibilities

* Vacuum, wipe down, and detail vehicles to company standards.

* Work efficiently in a fast-paced environment.

* Maintain a clean and organized workspace.

* Provide friendly and professional customer service.

* Work as part of a team to complete vehicles efficiently.

* Must be hardworking, dependable, and punctual.
