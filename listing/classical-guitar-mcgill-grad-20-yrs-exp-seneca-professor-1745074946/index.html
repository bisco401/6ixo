About this listing

In 4-6 sessions you'll be reading real music & playing your first Royal Conservatory pieces. Not fumbling through tabs - but ergonomically playing. That's what structured, one-on-one lessons with a real classical musician and educator can do. And it happens faster than you think.

I'm Matija Krunic. I hold a McGill Composition and Classical Guitar degree - a program that accepts only 6 students / year. I currently teach as a Professor at Seneca College.

BlogTO ranked me the #1 guitar teacher in Toronto. I've been teaching for 20 years.

But here's what matters to you: I build a custom lesson plan around your goals, your pace, and the repertoire you actually want to play.

What I teach: Technique and proper form - I demonstrate everything hands-on so you see and hear exactly how it should feel. Theory and analysis that connects directly to what you hear, so you can make expressive performance decisions - whether it's a simple waltz, Tango or a complex piece by Mozart or Beethoven.…
