About this listing

Selling my Dol-Fyn automatic water distiller. It’s in excellent condition and saw very little use. Bought new in Vancouver. A comparable system costs $2,600+ CAD new today.

This is not a countertop distiller. It’s a free-standing, commercial-grade, all-stainless system made in the USA by West Bend / Regal Ware. It’s built to run for decades. Dimensions are 36" H x 16" W X 11" Deep

Distilled water right at your kitchen tap

No lugging jugs or pails. The reservoir has a side outlet made for a demand pump. Add a pump, run a food-grade water line up to a small faucet on your countertop, and you’ve got pure distilled water on tap whenever you want it. That’s exactly how I had mine set up, and it’s the best way to use this unit. Pump kits are easy to find and simple to install. (Pump, tubing and faucet are not included.)
