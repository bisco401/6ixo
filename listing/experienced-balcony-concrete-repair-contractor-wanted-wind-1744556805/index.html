We are looking for an experienced individual contractor or small crew to repair exterior balconies at an apartment building near the University of Windsor.

Scope of Work:

Inspect and identify water infiltration points

Repair damaged or spalling concrete

Apply appropriate rust-resistant primer and protective coatings

Perform other necessary minor repairs

We prefer an experienced independent contractor or small crew rather than a large construction company.
