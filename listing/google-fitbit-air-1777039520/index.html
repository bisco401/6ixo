GOOGLE FITBIT AIR

Less on your wrist.More insight into your day.

Fitbit Air keeps activity, heart-rate and sleep tracking quietly in the background. Its screenless design leaves the wrist free of on-screen distractions, while your progress and insights are available in the Google Health app on your phone.

MOVE

See the activity that adds up.

Review steps, distance, calories burned and cardio load in the app, bringing everyday movement into a single view.

REST

Look back on your night.

Wear it during sleep to review sleep stages and build a clearer picture of your routine over time.

RECHARGE

Spend less time plugged in.

Battery life of up to a week supports everyday wear, with quick charging for a useful top-up when time is short.

Your phone is part of the experience.

A compatible phone, Google Account and Google Health app are required. Some advanced insights and coaching require Google Health Premium; features and service availability vary by region.
