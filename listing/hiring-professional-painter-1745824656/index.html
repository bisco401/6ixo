About this listing

We are looking for a skilled, driven, and dependable Painter to join our team! If you take pride in top-quality workmanship, move efficiently, and thrive in high-energy environments, we want to hear from you.

Job Responsibilities

Prepare surfaces (patching, sanding, caulking, priming, and taping) to ensure a flawless finish.

Apply paints, stains, and protective coatings using brushes, rollers, and spray equipment with precision.

Maintain a clean, organized, and safe job site at all times.

Deliver exceptional results while meeting strict project deadlines in a high-demand settings.

*Requirements & Qualifications

Experience: Minimum of 5 years of professional painting experience.

Transportation: Must have your own reliable vehicle for travel between job sites.

Must hold a valid driver’s license.

Tools: Must provide your own standard hand tools.

Work Ethic: Highly motivated attitude with the proven ability to perform exceptionally well in extremely fast-paced environments.
