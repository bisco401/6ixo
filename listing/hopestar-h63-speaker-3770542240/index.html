HOPESTAR H63 Portable Bluetooth Speaker – Waterproof, Powerful Sound

The HOPESTAR H63 Portable Bluetooth Speaker delivers crisp treble and deep bass with dual 20W speakers, making it perfect for outdoor adventures, beach trips, parties, and mobile use in Guyana. With Bluetooth 5.0, the H63 ensures fast and stable wireless connections to smartphones, tablets, and laptops, while TWS (True Wireless Stereo) allows pairing two units for an immersive 360° sound experience.

Built for durability, the IPX6 waterproof and dustproof shell protects the speaker from splashes, rain, or dust during outdoor activities. With multiple playback options—Bluetooth, TF card, AUX, and USB—the H63 combines flexibility, portability, and performance in one stylish package.
