About this listing

GTB Trucking Inc. is looking for a Company Long haul CDN/US truck driver. Heavy Haul and/or over dimensional experience considered a major asset with step deck and flat deck experience.

Pay Rate: Mileage based pay. Hourly paid short haul.

Responsibilities include Loading and unloading of goods, ensuring safety and security of cargo. Must be organized and be able to manage paperwork and fill out bills of lading. Receive and relay information to central dispatcher. Understand weights and dimensions, journey management and time management.

Educational requirement: Driver's License (Class 1 or A), Air Brake (Z) Endorsement. Clean 5-year commercial drivers abstract.

Related experience: Must be willing, able and confident to drive through mountain terrain and cross border drive (USA). Minimum 1 Years flatbed driving experience minimum, Tarping of loads experience, strapping and chaining of loads experience, over dimensional experience.

Benefits: Bonuses, Mileage Paid.
