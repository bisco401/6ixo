About this listing

We bring professional equipment and premium products straight to your driveway or parkade to give your ride that clean, fresh feel again.

(Note: We specialize strictly in cleaning and interior/exterior restoration; we do not do painting or bodywork.)

Our Simple Services

• Interior Deep Clean: Thorough vacuuming, full wipe down and sanitizing of all surfaces (dash, console, cup holders), glass cleaning, and extraction to lift out summer/winter salt stains.

• Exterior Wash & Shine: Safe hand and pressure wash and dry, deep wheel and tire cleaning, plus a high-gloss protective wax coating to shield your paint.

• The Full Detail (Best Value): Complete inside and out deep clean, including door jambs and a light engine bay refresh.

Note: Basic pet hair is included! However, heavy/severe pet hair requires specialized tools and extra time, we add extra fees depending on the condition. We will always give you a clear, upfront quote before starting.
