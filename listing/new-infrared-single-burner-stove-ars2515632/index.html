About this listing

A single burner infrared stove uses advanced radiant technology to pass heat directly to your food via electromagnetic waves. Unlike induction cooktops, infrared stoves work with all types of cookware, including glass, aluminum, ceramic, and copper. They heat up rapidly, transfer energy with up to 90% thermal efficiency, and are ideal for dorms, RVs, camping, or as an extra holiday burner..

Key Benefits

Universal Cookware Compatibility: Works seamlessly with any flat-bottomed pot or pan you already own.

Rapid Heating: Reaches exceptionally high cooking temperatures much faster than traditional coil stoves.

Precise Low Simmering: Delivers steady, consistent radiant heat that is perfect for delicate sauces or slow-cooked stews.

Easy Cleanup: Features a smooth, flat glass-ceramic surface that wipes clean quickly..
