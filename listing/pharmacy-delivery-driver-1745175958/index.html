PHARMACY DELIVERY DRIVER NEEDED – SIDE INCOME OPPORTUNITY

Looking for a way to earn extra money on the side? This could be the perfect opportunity for you!

Delivery Route – 3 Locations

You will pick up prescriptions and deliveries from the following locations, completing each store’s deliveries before moving on to the next:

1. Shoppers Drug Mart – 3010 Bloor Street West

2. Shoppers Drug Mart – 2155 St. Clair Avenue West

3. Metro Pharmacy – 5097 Wilson Avenue

* Approximately 20–30 deliveries per day

* Occasionally, payments must be collected from customers
