About this listing

The Polycom VVX 401 IP Phone is a 12-line mid-range business VoIP phone featuring a color display:

Key Specifications & Features

• Display: 3.5-inch backlit color LCD (320 x 240 resolution).

• Line Support: Up to 12 line appearances or Busy Lamp Field (BLF) keys.

• Audio: Polycom HD Voice technology and Acoustic Clarity technology.

• Network Ports: Dual 10/100 Ethernet ports (LAN and PC pass-through). (Note: Unlike the VVX 411 model, the VVX 401 does not feature Gigabit ports).

• USB & Accessories: Single USB 2.0 port for media/storage, RJ-9 headset port, and support for optional color or paper expansion modules.

• Power: Built-in auto-sensing PoE (Power over Ethernet, IEEE 802.3af) or optional 48V DC power supply (sold separately).

Common Administration & Default Settings

• Default Admin Password: 456
