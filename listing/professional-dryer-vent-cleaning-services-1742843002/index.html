A CLOGGED DRYER VENT IS MORE THAN AN INCONVENIENCE

Every time you use your dryer, lint and debris can accumulate inside the dryer vent system. Even when you clean your lint screen after every load, lint can still travel deeper into the vent.

A restricted vent can reduce airflow, make your dryer work harder, increase drying times, and allow heat to build up.

WHY REGULAR DRYER VENT CLEANING IS IMPORTANT

✅ Helps reduce fire risk

Lint is highly combustible. Keeping the vent system clean helps reduce the accumulation of flammable lint and debris.

✅ Improves airflow

A clean vent allows moist, warm air to escape more efficiently.

✅ Can reduce drying times

When airflow is restricted, your clothes may take much longer to dry. Properly maintained ventilation can help your dryer operate more efficiently.
