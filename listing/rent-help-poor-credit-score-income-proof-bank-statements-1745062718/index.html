Denied a rental? Low income or having trouble proving income?

RENTEDGE helps renters strengthen their applications (Credit score + Income proof documents) and improve their chances of finding a home.

Contact us if any of these apply - (we can fix your problem):

• Poor Equifax credit score or issues with your credit report

• Missing or limited proof of income
