About this listing

SAMSUNG 15W ADAPTER The Samsung 15W USB-C Power Adapter (e. g., model EP-T2510) is a single-port wall adapter designed to deliver Super Fast Charging to compatible Galaxy phones and other devices. It is the recommended charger for most models in the Galaxy S, Galaxy Note, and Galaxy A series. Super Fast Charging (SFC): This is Samsung's name for their fast-charging protocol, which is based on the industry-standard USB Power Delivery (PD) 3.0 with the crucial addition of PPS (Programmable Power Supply) . PPS Technology: PPS is what truly enables the maximum 15W speed. It allows the charger and the device to continuously communicate and fine-tune the voltage and current in real-time. This dynamic adjustment leads to faster, more efficient charging and better heat management.
