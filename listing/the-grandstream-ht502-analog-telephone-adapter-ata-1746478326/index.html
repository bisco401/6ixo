About this listing

The Grandstream HT502 analog telephone adapter (ATA) allows users to create a high-quality and manageable IP telephony solution for home or office applications. The product features 2 FXS ports, an integrated NAT router and dual 10/100Mbps Ethernet WAN and LAN ports to enable a shared broadband connection between multiple Ethernet devices as well as the extension of VoIP services to analog phones.

In addition to being SIP compliant, the product features support for up to 2 SIP account profiles, excellent voice quality, and advanced telephony features.
