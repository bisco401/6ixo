**TRYING TO MATCH AN OLD WOOD FLOOR? RECLAIMED & OLD GROWTH WOOD**

Looking for wood flooring to match an existing floor? We may be able to help.

American Reclaimed Wood Floors is a family-owned business supplying custom-milled reclaimed and old growth flooring.

Whether you are adding onto an existing floor, restoring an older home or looking for a particular wood species, we can help you explore your options.

We offer Reclaimed Heart Pine, White Oak, Old Growth Douglas Fir, Thermally Modified Wood, wide plank solid and engineered flooring, beams, stair treads and trim.

Custom widths, lengths and surface finishes available depending on the material.
