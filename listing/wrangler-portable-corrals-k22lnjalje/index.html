Wrangler Portable Corral

The Wrangler Portable Corral is the most versatile portable corral system available. Whether you want to catch and load large groups of cattle or work them right where they are, the Wrangler has all the features you need. Features include: Easy to use Electric over Hydraulic Controls, Reinforced Hydraulic Lift System, Solar Charging Panel, Solid Tires on Panels, Adjustable Alley, and Built-in Palpation Door. The Wrangler Portable Corral uses a hydraulic lift system to raise and lower the corral eliminating the need for you to lift panels, wind cables, or crank jacks to put the system in place. With 3 sizes to choose from, you can match the right corral and options to fit your unique operation’s needs.
