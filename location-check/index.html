Check your device location

This asks your browser directly for your location. It helps identify why Allow isn’t working on 6ixo.

This check does not send your coordinates to 6ixo or a city lookup provider. It does not save them.

For Safari on Mac, check Safari in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. Then allow 6ixo.com under Safari → Settings → Websites → Location.
