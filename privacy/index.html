1. Scope and who is responsible

This Privacy Policy applies to the 6ixo website, mobile-friendly web experience, future 6ixo mobile applications, and related marketplace, messaging, booking, community and support services (collectively, the “Services”).

6ixo is responsible for the personal information described in this Policy. Some website features, including dating-related features, may not be offered in the downloadable mobile applications; when those features are used on the website, this Policy still applies.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account and profile information: name, email address, age, password credentials handled by our authentication provider, public username, phone number, city, region, country, biography, interests, photos, videos and account preferences.
  • Listings and other user content: listing titles, descriptions, prices, locations, images, videos, item or vehicle details, availability, reviews, community posts, rewards posts and other content you choose to publish.
  • Messages and safety information: in-app messages, support communications, reports, blocks, moderation records and information supplied during a dispute or safety review.
  • Bookings and transactions: booking dates, guest and host information, contact details, totals, currency, transaction status, payout or promotion information and identifiers associated with Stripe transactions.
  • Rental and host verification: driver name, driver-licence number and issuing region, host application details, identification or supporting documents, insurance information, and eligibility or safety-related responses when those features are used.
  • Contact requests: your name, email address, subject and message when you contact us.

Information collected from your device

  • Location: precise device location only when you grant permission, plus city, region or country that you enter manually. Approximate location may also be inferred from an IP address by service providers.
  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser or app type, device information, referring page, pages viewed, interactions, timestamps, diagnostics and similar usage information. Optional analytics is described below.
  • Local device storage: sign-in state, privacy choices, saved items, filters, drafts, messages, reports, preferences and similar features may be stored in cookies or local browser/app storage.

Information from other sources

We may receive information from payment, authentication, mapping, email, fraud-prevention and hosting providers. The marketplace may also display listing information supplied by partners or obtained from publicly available listing sources where collection and display are permitted.

Please do not post sensitive information publicly. Avoid placing an exact home address, government identification number, financial information or other unnecessary sensitive information in a public profile, listing, message subject or review.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • create and secure accounts, authenticate users and maintain profiles;
  • publish, search, rank and recommend listings or other content;
  • enable messages, bookings, payments, promotions and customer support;
  • provide nearby results, maps and location-based features when requested;
  • review hosts, prevent fraud, moderate content, investigate reports and protect people and the Services;
  • send service communications such as confirmations, security alerts and booking updates;
  • measure and improve performance when optional analytics has been accepted; and
  • comply with law, enforce our rules and establish or defend legal claims.

Where applicable, our legal grounds are your consent, performance of a contract with you, compliance with legal obligations, and our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the Services. You may withdraw consent for optional processing at any time, without affecting processing that already occurred lawfully.

4. When we share information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. We may disclose information in these circumstances:

  • Other users and the public: information you deliberately publish, and the limited profile, booking or contact details needed for people to transact or communicate.
  • Service providers: Supabase for authentication, databases, storage and server functions; Stripe for payments; Google services for optional analytics and mapping; and providers supporting hosting, content delivery, email, security and customer support.
  • Legal and safety reasons: when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, prevent fraud, protect safety or enforce our policies.
  • Business changes: in connection with a financing, reorganization, merger, sale or transfer, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal safeguards.
  • With your direction: when you ask us to share information or give separate consent.

Payment-card details entered into Stripe’s payment interface are processed by Stripe. 6ixo receives transaction identifiers, amounts and payment status, but does not need to receive the full card number.

5. Public content and communications

Listings, public profiles, photos, videos, reviews, ratings, community posts and other content you publish are designed to be seen by other people and may be copied or reshared outside 6ixo. Messages are intended for their participants, but may be reviewed when reported or when reasonably necessary for safety, support, fraud prevention or legal compliance.

Removing content or deleting an account does not necessarily remove copies already saved by other people, lawfully retained transaction records, or content that has been de-identified so it can no longer reasonably be linked to you.

6. Cookies, local storage and analytics

6ixo uses necessary cookies and local storage for authentication, security, preview access, preferences, saved items and other requested functionality. These technologies are required for parts of the Services to work.

Google Analytics is optional. It may collect device, usage and approximate-location information to help us understand how the Services perform. We do not load Google Analytics until you choose “Accept analytics.” Choosing “Only necessary” does not affect core marketplace functionality.

You can change this choice at any time. Browser settings can also block or delete cookies, although doing so may reset preferences or sign you out.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including providing the Services, maintaining security, resolving disputes and meeting legal, tax, accounting or reporting obligations.

  • Account and profile information is generally retained while the account is active and deleted or de-identified after a valid deletion request, subject to limited backup, fraud-prevention and legal retention.
  • Listings, messages, reviews and other user content are retained while needed to provide the feature and may be retained for a limited period after removal for safety, moderation or dispute purposes.
  • Booking, transaction and payment records may be retained for the period required by tax, accounting, chargeback, anti-fraud and other applicable laws.
  • Host-verification and driver information is retained only for as long as needed to review eligibility, support an active booking, address safety or disputes, and meet legal requirements.
  • Analytics information is retained according to our configured provider settings and is deleted or aggregated when no longer needed.

Residual copies may remain temporarily in encrypted or access-controlled backups until those backups are overwritten through the normal cycle.

8. Security and international processing

We use administrative, technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including encrypted network connections, authentication, access controls and restricted storage for verification documents. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a unique password and report suspected misuse promptly.

6ixo and its service providers may process information in Canada, the United States and other countries. Information processed outside your home country may be subject to the laws and lawful-access rules of that location. We use contractual and other safeguards where required.

9. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction of your personal information; object to certain processing; withdraw consent; or appeal a decision. You may also complain to the privacy or data-protection authority in your jurisdiction.

  • Update ordinary profile information from your profile screen.
  • Decline or revoke location, camera, photo, video and notification access in browser or device settings.
  • Change optional analytics through “Privacy choices.”
  • Request account and associated-data deletion through our deletion page.
  • Email the Privacy Officer for access, correction, export, objection or other privacy requests.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right. Certain information may be retained where permitted or required for transactions, fraud prevention, safety, legal compliance or legal claims.

Request account deletion Email the Privacy Officer

10. Age restrictions and children’s privacy

6ixo accounts and interactive features are intended only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account or submitted personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the Services, providers or legal requirements change. We will post the revised version here, update the effective date and provide additional notice when a change materially affects your rights or how we use personal information.
