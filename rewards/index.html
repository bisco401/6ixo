What to do when you have information

Read the full report

Compare the description, location, timeline and source before deciding whether your information is relevant.

Use official channels

For emergencies, crimes or missing people, contact the appropriate authorities rather than investigating yourself.

Protect private details

Share credible observations without publishing sensitive personal information or making accusations.

Important safety note

Never confront or pursue anyone.

6ixo community posts are user-submitted and are not a replacement for police, emergency services, animal services or other official agencies.

  • Call local emergency services when there is immediate danger.
  • Do not send money, disclose financial information or accept suspicious reward arrangements.
  • Report false, harmful or privacy-violating posts and preserve relevant evidence.
