Marketplace & Dating Safety

Practical checks before paying, sharing information, booking or meeting someone on 6ixo.

1. Verify the offer and the person

Slow down when money, documents, vehicles, property or a personal meeting is involved. Inspect the item or use a credible independent inspection. Ask for current details and check that the person is authorized to sell, rent or provide it. A profile, review, badge or featured placement is not a guarantee.

Compare the complete price, currency, taxes, deposit, delivery, cancellation and recurring charges. Keep messages and written terms. Imported offers may be stale: confirm availability and price with the linked source.

2. Recognize payment scams

  • Be suspicious of implausibly low prices, urgency, refusal to allow inspection, payments to an unrelated person, or requests for gift cards, cryptocurrency or an unexplained wire.
  • Do not refund an alleged overpayment until your financial institution has verified the original payment. A screenshot or email receipt is not proof of cleared funds.
  • Never share passwords, one-time codes, recovery links, PINs or remote device access. Open 6ixo or your payment provider directly rather than following an unexpected login link.
  • For general marketplace purchases, choose an appropriate payment method with records and fraud protection. For eligible platform bookings or promotions, use the payment flow and retain the booking or Stripe reference.

See the payment Terms and rental cancellation rules. Do not assume a direct purchase has platform payment protection.

3. Meet safely and respect consent

Choose a suitable public or professionally supervised place, tell someone your plans and arrange your own transport. Avoid carrying unnecessary cash or handing over your identity documents. You can decline or end a meeting at any time.

For dating or companionship, a match, paid feature or gift is not consent. Do not send unwanted sexual material or continue contacting someone who asks you to stop. Do not share your exact home, hotel or live whereabouts publicly. Read the dating and consent rules.

4. Check property and vehicle bookings

For a property, verify the address and the host’s authority, confirm check-in, occupancy, facilities and cancellation terms, and document relevant differences. Do not pay a deposit for a property that cannot be credibly verified. Review the Short-Term Rental Guidelines for host standards, guest conduct, cameras, property access and damage disputes.

For a vehicle, inspect condition, photograph handover and return, confirm licence requirements, pickup times, included distance, deposits and charges. Review the actual host-provided insurance policy, exclusions and deductible. 6ixo does not currently provide vehicle-rental insurance or a protection plan.

Keep the confirmed financial terms and note the property or vehicle’s time zone. Current eligible platform bookings use a full-refund cutoff 24 hours before check-in or pickup; late cancellations and trip issues need support review. The terms recorded for your booking and mandatory rights still apply.

5. Report a concern

Use Report on the relevant profile, listing or chat where available, or email contact@6ixo.com with “Safety report” in the subject. Include the listing or account reference, what happened, when and relevant non-sensitive records. You can block unwanted contact.

If your own listing or account is restricted, follow the appeals process. Reported activity is assessed against the rules; filing a report does not guarantee removal or a particular outcome.

6. Immediate danger, financial loss or identity theft

Contact local emergency services if someone is in immediate danger. 6ixo is not an emergency response or continuously monitored safety service.

If money or identity information has been stolen, contact your bank or payment provider promptly, secure your account and report the incident to the appropriate local fraud or law-enforcement authority. Preserve records. A platform report does not replace a bank, payment-provider or police report and does not extend their deadlines.

For suspected child exploitation, report the account or URL without downloading, forwarding or attaching abusive material.
