Hire clearly

How to choose a service provider

Write a useful brief

Explain what you need, where it happens, your timing and any important constraints.

Compare more than price

Ask about experience, examples, credentials, insurance and who will perform the work.

Document the agreement

Confirm the scope, total price, deposit, materials, timeline and change process in writing.

Hire with care

Verify the provider

  • Check licensing, insurance or permits when the work in your location requires them.
  • Do not share access, sensitive documents or large deposits before trust is established.
  • For major jobs, use a written agreement and keep a record of payments and changes.
