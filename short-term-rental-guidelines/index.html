1. When these rules apply

These requirements apply to short-term accommodation offered through 6ixo and to hosts, booking guests and the people they bring. The booking guest must be an adult and must explain the applicable house rules to their group.

Read these rules with the Terms of Use, Listing Rules and Community Guidelines. A host’s additional rules must be lawful, disclosed before booking and consistent with these policies. Later policy or listing edits do not change a confirmed booking’s recorded financial terms. External bookings use the external provider’s payment and cancellation arrangements.

2. Lawful, accurate and safe hosting

  • Have the owner’s authority and meet the property’s registration, permit, occupancy, tax, insurance and building requirements. Display any registration number required by local law. Host approval does not replace those requirements.
  • Describe the actual address, private and shared rooms, sleeping arrangements, guest limit, amenities, accessibility, availability and full price. Explain restricted amenities, nearby construction, significant noise, on-site occupants and relevant hazards before booking. Do not edit photographs to conceal damage or invent features.
  • Maintain safe electrical and heating equipment, working smoke alarms, required carbon monoxide alarms, a fire extinguisher and clear escape routes. Provide emergency instructions and the property address to guests. Disclose pools, heights and other material risks; address preventable hazards before accepting guests.

3. Cleaning and readiness

Prepare the property between every stay. Supply freshly cleaned bedding and towels, clean bathrooms and food preparation areas, empty rubbish bins and remove waste left by previous occupants. Check that advertised facilities work and that the home is free from infestations, significant mould and other health hazards.

The agreed cleaning fee covers normal preparation between stays. State any reasonable checkout tasks before booking; do not impose surprise chores or penalties for unfinished routine cleaning. Guests must dispose of rubbish appropriately and avoid leaving damage or a condition that requires exceptional cleaning beyond ordinary use. Document concerns under the damage and extra-charge rules.

4. Reservations and check-in

Hosts must keep calendars current and honour accepted stays. Provide the booked property and amenities; obtain the guest’s written agreement before changing a confirmed stay’s property, dates, price or material conditions. A replacement property is not an automatic substitute for the original booking.

Send correct arrival directions, the agreed check-in window, working keys or access codes and a contact for access problems in time for arrival. Keep access available throughout the stay. Arrange a reachable backup when you cannot help at check-in. Share access codes privately and change them when appropriate between guests.

If hosting becomes impossible, tell the guest and contact 6ixo promptly with the booking reference and reason. Never pressure a guest to cancel on your behalf or misstate the cause to avoid responsibility. Keep any agreed refund or compensation in writing and honour it through the appropriate payment process.

5. Host communication

Hosts must monitor booking messages or arrange a responsible co-host. Response expectations use the property’s local time:

  • More than five days before arrival: reply to booking questions within 24 hours.
  • Within five days of arrival and during a stay: reply to routine questions within 12 hours.
  • For failed access or a serious stay problem reported between 8 a.m. and 10 p.m.: acknowledge within one hour and explain the next action. During an agreed check-in window, provide a reachable access contact even outside those hours.

Guests should share arrival changes and respond to reasonable questions needed to resolve a problem. Keep important agreements in the booking conversation. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

6. Guest conduct and house rules

  • People and gatherings: stay within the booked occupancy and legal capacity. Ask before bringing additional visitors. Open-invitation parties and gatherings that cause disruption, damage or a safety risk are prohibited.
  • Noise: observe disclosed quiet hours and avoid disturbing neighbours at any time.
  • Arrival and departure: use the agreed check-in and checkout times, return keys and leave belongings only with permission. Arrange extensions in writing.
  • Smoking and animals: follow disclosed smoking, vaping and pet restrictions. Obtain approval for pets and their number. Required service-animal accommodations take precedence over pet rules.
  • Property: respect private or restricted areas. Do not remove belongings, tamper with alarms or locks, or use facilities in an unsafe way.
  • Other uses: obtain written permission for commercial filming, photography or an event. Never use a stay for unlawful activity, threats or harassment.

Hosts must disclose house rules before booking. New restrictions require agreement; host rules cannot remove mandatory rights or authorize intrusive monitoring.

7. Cameras and monitoring devices

Hidden cameras and devices used to secretly record guests are prohibited. Hosts may not install or keep cameras or recording devices that monitor indoor accommodation, including shared living areas, hallways, bedrooms and bathrooms. This applies even when a device is switched off or disconnected.

Exterior cameras and doorbell cameras are allowed only where lawful. Before booking, disclose each device’s location and the areas it covers in the listing. Do not aim them into interior spaces or outdoor areas used for bathing, changing or other private activities. A guest’s acceptance of a listing does not permit otherwise prohibited surveillance.

Noise monitors may measure sound levels only, without recording conversations. Disclose them before booking and keep them out of bedrooms, bathrooms and sleeping areas. Disclose other smart devices with recording functions, explain how guests can turn those functions off, and never use them to monitor guests secretly. Hosts remain responsible for lawful access to and retention of any permitted recordings.

8. Privacy and property access

Hosts, managers and maintenance workers must obtain the guest’s permission for entry into an exclusively rented space during a stay, except for an immediate emergency or access specifically required by applicable law. Explain the purpose, timing and people entering. Merely announcing a visit does not establish permission.

Identify shared spaces and host-only areas in advance. In shared accommodation, respect each person’s private room and an occupied bathroom. Guests must also obtain permission before entering private host or occupant spaces or using their personal belongings.

Use guest contact and identification information only for legitimate booking or legal requirements, collect only what is needed and keep it secure. Explain any legally required identification process before booking. Do not publish guest documents, private conversations, access codes or travel details, or ask guests to send payment-card details or passwords.

9. Accessibility and fair treatment

Describe accessibility features accurately, including steps, entrance widths, lifts and bathroom arrangements where relevant. Do not describe a space as accessible without explaining the features available.

Hosts must follow applicable non-discrimination and disability accommodation requirements, including required service-animal access. Pet bans or pet charges cannot override a mandatory accommodation. Discuss practical needs respectfully and privately; do not demand public disclosure of a diagnosis. Contact support if an accommodation or house rule creates a concern.

10. Damage, deposits and extra charges

Disclose any permitted deposit, its amount, how it is held, possible deductions and the return arrangements before a guest commits. Do not add undisclosed mandatory charges after confirmation or demand an outside payment to secure access to a platform-booked stay.

Guests must report accidental damage promptly. Hosts seeking compensation must identify the damage or exceptional cleaning, its connection to the stay and the reasonable amount claimed, with dated photographs and receipts or estimates. Ordinary wear, routine turnover cleaning and invented penalties are not damage claims.

Give the guest the evidence and an opportunity to respond. Keep requests in writing and use support for a disputed platform booking. A report or accusation alone does not authorize a card charge or automatic deposit deduction. 6ixo does not promise reimbursement, insurance or replacement accommodation under these guidelines.

11. Problems, cancellations and refunds

If a stay is unavailable, inaccessible, unsafe, significantly unclean or materially different from its description, contact the host when safe and report the issue to contact@6ixo.com as soon as reasonably possible. Include the booking reference, what happened, dates and relevant photographs or messages. Hosts must cooperate with the review and take reasonable steps to address the problem.

For eligible 6ixo bookings, the rental Terms and confirmed booking record control payments and cancellation deadlines. The current full-refund cutoff is 24 hours before check-in. Reported problems and late cancellations receive support review; the cutoff does not prevent reporting a safety or delivery problem or exercising mandatory rights.

Explain the requested remedy and retain receipts. We review the evidence, booked terms and applicable rights before deciding an available correction or eligible refund. A report does not guarantee a particular payment outcome. Bank, payment-provider and legal deadlines still apply.

12. Reports, enforcement and appeals

Use Report on the listing, profile or conversation where available, or email contact@6ixo.com with “Stay safety report” and the relevant references. Neighbours may report noise or other safety concerns using the listing reference or location without publishing guests’ private details. Threats or retaliation for a complaint or honest review are prohibited.

Under our reporting and appeals process, action may include requiring a correction or proof that a hazard was resolved, warnings, listing removal, feature restrictions or account suspension. Serious safety or privacy violations may require immediate action. Booking refunds and transfer holds follow the applicable rental Terms and payment review.

To appeal, email “Moderation appeal” with the decision, account or listing reference and your explanation or new evidence. We aim to acknowledge emailed reports and appeals within three business days. 6ixo is not an emergency response service or a continuously monitored stay support service.
