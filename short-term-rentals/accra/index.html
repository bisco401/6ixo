Frequently asked questions

Short-Term Rentals questions for Accra

Where should I search for a short-term rental in Accra?

The best area depends on your plans. Consider proximity and travel time to the airport, work, family, events and the places you expect to visit.

Can I find furnished monthly apartments in Accra?

Some hosts may offer weekly or monthly rates. Confirm which utilities, internet, cleaning and deposits are included.

What amenities should I confirm?

Ask about electricity and backup power, water, internet, air conditioning or fans, kitchen facilities, laundry, security and parking.

How do I avoid rental scams?

Verify the host and property, review current details, keep communication documented and avoid pressure to use an unverified payment method.
