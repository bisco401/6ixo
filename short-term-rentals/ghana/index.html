Frequently asked questions

Short-Term Rentals questions for Ghana

What kinds of short-term rentals can I find in Ghana?

Listings may include furnished apartments, homes, guest suites and private rooms. Available property types depend on the destination and dates.

Are monthly furnished rentals available?

Some hosts may offer weekly or monthly stays. Ask what utilities, cleaning, internet and deposits are included in the longer-stay price.

How should I verify a rental before paying?

Check the host and property details, request current information, review the written terms and avoid payment arrangements you cannot verify.

What should I ask about before arrival?

Confirm the address process, check-in time, access, utilities, internet, security, parking, house rules and emergency contact.
